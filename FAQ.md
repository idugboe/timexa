# Timexa (TMX) FAQ

## Is TMX live?

No. The proposed Timexa smart contract is designed as a fixed-supply ERC-20 token with no additional minting function and with ownership-management functionality. The source code has been compiled locally, but the contract has not been deployed to a blockchain. No live TMX token contract currently exists, and the contract has not undergone an independent security audit. The proposed allocations are not enforced by the contract.

## How do I claim TMX?

There is no active airdrop and no claim is open. Round 1 is a proposed distribution plan for 1,000 TMX per eligible wallet, subject to a maximum of 5,000 wallets. Eligibility criteria, the final participant list, and distribution arrangements remain subject to confirmation. No tokens have been distributed, and publication of this plan does not guarantee participation or receipt. Never pay anyone to claim TMX or to be added to a whitelist.

## What is the contract address?

There is no contract address because TMX has not been deployed. If an address is created, it will be published only on the official website and official project channels: https://idugboe.github.io/timexa/. Do not trust an address sent by an unsolicited account or posted on an unofficial site.

## How do I spot fakes?

Be cautious of unsolicited direct messages, payment requests for a claim or whitelist spot, and non-official websites. Round 1 is only a proposed distribution plan, not an active airdrop; no tokens have been distributed and no claims are open. Check the official Timexa website and official project channels for announcements and any future contract address. Do not connect your wallet or share sensitive information with a site or person you cannot verify.

## What are the proposed token allocations?

The proposed distribution plan is 40% airdrop (400,000,000 TMX), 30% liquidity (300,000,000 TMX), 20% team (200,000,000 TMX), and 10% community (100,000,000 TMX), based on a fixed total supply of 1,000,000,000 TMX. These percentages are not enforced by the current contract. If deployed in its present form, the entire initial supply will be minted to the deployer's address. Any subsequent distribution depends on the project executing transfers. The percentages do not establish that tokens have been distributed, reserved in separate wallets, or locked.

## What is the team allocation policy?

The proposed team allocation is 20% (200,000,000 TMX). It is not enforced by the current contract. If deployed in its present form, the entire initial supply will mint to the deployer's address. The proposed allocation does not establish that tokens are separately held or locked, and any distribution depends on the project executing transfers. Any plans for how the team allocation may be handled are proposals only, not completed on-chain actions or guarantees.

## How do I stay updated?

Use the official Timexa website and official project channels for updates: https://idugboe.github.io/timexa/. Verify any shared contract address there. Do not rely on unsolicited messages or unofficial sites.

## Is participation or receipt guaranteed?

No. Round 1 is proposed, not active. The proposed amount is 1,000 TMX per eligible wallet, up to 5,000 wallets, but eligibility criteria, the final participant list, and distribution arrangements remain subject to confirmation. No tokens have been distributed, no claims are open, and publication of the plan does not guarantee participation or receipt. More generally, there is no guarantee of profit, token value, liquidity, or exchange listing.
