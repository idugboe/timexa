# Timexa (TMX) Whitepaper

## Project overview

The proposed Timexa smart contract is designed as a fixed-supply ERC-20 token with no additional minting function and with ownership-management functionality. The source code has been compiled locally, but the contract has not been deployed to a blockchain. Consequently, no live TMX token contract currently exists. The proposed token allocations are not enforced by the contract, and the contract has not undergone an independent security audit.

These percentages represent the project's proposed distribution plan and are not enforced by the current smart contract. If the contract is deployed in its present form, the entire initial supply will be minted to the deployer's address. Any subsequent distribution will depend on the project executing the relevant transfers. The proposed allocation percentages do not establish that tokens have been distributed, reserved in separate wallets, or locked.

Round 1 is a proposed distribution plan, not an active airdrop. The proposed amount is 1,000 TMX per eligible wallet, subject to a maximum of 5,000 wallets. Eligibility criteria, the final participant list, and distribution arrangements remain subject to confirmation. No tokens have been distributed, no claims are open, and publication of this plan does not guarantee participation or receipt of tokens.

## Distribution plan

The proposed process is to select eligible wallets through a whitelist and send the relevant TMX directly to those wallets. This is a proposed plan, not an active airdrop; eligibility criteria, the final participant list, and distribution arrangements remain subject to confirmation. No tokens have been distributed, no claims are open, and the plan does not guarantee participation or receipt. Any distribution depends on the project executing transfers. The current contract does not enforce the proposed allocations or whitelist process.

No contract address exists yet. If a contract is created, its address will be published only on the official Timexa website and official project channels. Do not rely on addresses shared in direct messages or on unofficial sites.
